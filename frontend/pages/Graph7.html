Graph Explanation

This donut chart titled "Distribution Across Nationality" visualizes the diversity of passenger nationalities in the airline dataset. Created using Plotly Express with a hole parameter of 0.5 for the donut effect, the chart displays the top 20 nationalities by passenger count. Each slice represents a nationality, with the slice size proportional to the number of passengers from that nationality. The data is preprocessed by counting passengers by nationality, selecting the top 20, and sorting them in descending order for clear presentation. This visualization provides quick insights into the most represented nationalities among airline passengers, helping understand the international composition of the customer base and identify key demographic groups in terms of passenger nationality.
