Graph Explanation

This line plot titled "Flight Status Based on Departure Date" tracks the temporal variation of flight counts across three status categories: On Time, Delayed, and Cancelled. Created using Seaborn's lineplot with departure dates on the x-axis and flight counts on the y-axis, the visualization uses different colored lines (Set2 palette) to distinguish between flight statuses. The data is aggregated by grouping flights by both departure date and status, then counting occurrences for each combination. This time-series analysis reveals operational patterns, identifies peak travel periods, and shows how flight reliability varies over time. The legend clearly indicates which line corresponds to each flight status, making it easy to track trends and compare performance across different time periods.
