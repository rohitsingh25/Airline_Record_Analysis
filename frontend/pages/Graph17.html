Graph Explanation

This pie chart illustrates the proportional distribution of airline passengers among the top 10 countries in Europe. Each slice represents a country, with the slice size corresponding to the percentage of passengers from that country relative to the total European passenger count in the dataset. The percentage labels on each slice provide a quick and convenient way to assess each country's contribution to the overall European passenger traffic. By filtering for the European continent and selecting the top 10 countries by passenger volume, this visualization offers a clear and visually appealing representation of market share, helping identify the dominant players in European air travel and understand the regional distribution of passenger traffic across the continent.
