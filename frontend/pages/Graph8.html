Graph Explanation

This pie chart displays the distribution of flight statuses across the airline dataset, categorizing flights into three status types: "On Time," "Delayed," and "Cancelled." Generated using Plotly Express with a sequential RdBu color scheme, the visualization shows the count and percentage of flights in each status category. The size of each pie segment is proportional to the number of flights in that category, making it easy to compare the operational performance at a glance. This chart provides key insights into airline reliability and punctuality, helping stakeholders assess overall flight performance, identify areas for improvement, and understand the proportion of disrupted versus smooth operations in the dataset.
